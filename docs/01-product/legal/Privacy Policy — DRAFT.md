## 1. What this policy covers

E7GZ PS lets you find PlayStation and gaming venues, book a room, and join tournaments. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the E7GZ PS mobile app, why we collect it, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

Venues that use the E7GZ Venue Dashboard do so under their own agreement with E7GZ.

## 2. The data we collect

**You give us, when you create an account:**

- Full name
- Username
- Mobile phone number
- Email address
- Password (we store only a protected, one-way form of it; nobody at E7GZ can read your password)
- A referral code, if a friend gave you one

**Created when you use the app:**

- Your bookings and tournament entries: venue, room, date, time, price, status, and why a booking ended
- Attendance: whether you checked in, arrived late, or did not show up
- Your account standing: no-show count, bans, and Verified Player status
- Points and wallet history, and referrals you made
- Reviews you write and reports you send
- Notifications we sent you
- A record that you accepted this policy and the Terms of Use, and when

**From your phone:**

- Your location, only if you allow it, to show venues near you. We use it for that search and we do not keep a history of where you were.
- A push notification token, so we can send notifications to your phone
- Technical data needed to keep your account safe: sign-in times, the type of device, and the internet address (IP) of sign-in attempts

We do not ask for your contacts, photos, microphone or camera. We do not collect payment card details: at launch the app takes no payments.

## 3. Why we use your data

| Purpose | Data used |
|---|---|
| Create and protect your account, and check your phone number by SMS code | Name, username, phone, email, password, sign-in data |
| Make, show and manage your bookings and tournament entries | Account and booking data |
| Tell the venue who is coming | Username; your phone number only after the venue accepts your booking |
| Send you booking updates and reminders | Phone, email, push token |
| Keep the service fair: no-show rules, bans, Verified Player | Attendance and standing |
| Points, referrals and booking-fee refunds | Points, wallet and referral history |
| Show venues near you | Location, if you allow it |
| Prevent abuse and fraud, and fix problems | Sign-in data, technical data, history |
| Meet legal duties | As the law requires |

We use your data because it is needed to provide the service you asked for, because you agreed, or because the law requires it. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising by other companies.

## 4. Who sees your data

- **The venue you book with** sees your username and your booking. It sees your phone number only after it accepts your booking, so it can reach you about that booking.
- **E7GZ staff (Platform Admin)** can see your account and history to support you, handle complaints and correct mistakes. Their actions are recorded.
- **Other customers** see your username, never your name or phone number, on reviews you write, and in tournaments you join where the app shows the players.
- **Companies that work for us**, only as far as they need:
  - an SMS provider, to send verification codes to your phone: SMS Misr
  - Google Firebase and Apple, to deliver push notifications
  - an email provider, to send emails: [EMAIL PROVIDER NAME]
  - a hosting provider, where the service and its data run: [HOSTING PROVIDER AND COUNTRY]
  - Google Maps, to show the map
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.

Some of these companies may process or store data outside Egypt. We give each of them only the data it needs for its job, and we choose companies that protect personal data.

## 5. How long we keep your data

We keep your account data while your account exists.

You can ask to delete your account from inside the app at any time. E7GZ support then completes the deletion. When it is complete:

- your name, phone number, email and other identifying details are removed from your account, and you can no longer sign in;
- your sign-in sessions and push notification tokens are cancelled;
- your reviews are removed;
- records of past bookings and their history are kept without your identifying details, because venues and E7GZ need correct records of what happened.

We complete a deletion request within 30 days.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can:

- see and correct your profile details in the app;
- turn location and notifications on or off in your phone's settings; the app still works, but you will not see venues sorted by distance or receive push updates;
- ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to [CONTACT GMAIL — the Product Owner is creating it];
- delete your account from inside the app;
- complain to the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Centre if you believe your data is misused.

## 7. How we protect your data

We protect your data with measures such as encrypted connections, protected passwords, limited staff access, and records of sensitive actions. No system is perfectly safe; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.

## 8. Children

E7GZ PS is for people aged 16 and over. If you are 16 or 17, use it only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

## 9. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we publish the new version here with a new date. If the change is important, we tell you in the app and may ask you to agree again.

## 10. Contact

E7GZ-PS — [CONTACT GMAIL — the Product Owner is creating it] — El Banafseg 3, New Cairo, Egypt

- اسم المستخدم
- رقم الموبايل

**ما يُنشأ أثناء استخدامك للتطبيق:**

- حجوزاتك ومشاركاتك في البطولات: المكان والغرفة والتاريخ والوقت والسعر والحالة وسبب انتهاء الحجز
- الحضور: هل سجّلت حضورك، أو تأخرت، أو لم تحضر
- وضع حسابك: عدد مرات الغياب، وحالات المنع، وحالة اللاعب الموثَّق
- سجل النقاط والمحفظة، والإحالات التي قمت بها
- التقييمات التي تكتبها والبلاغات التي ترسلها
- الإشعارات التي أرسلناها لك
- سجلّ بأنك وافقت على هذه السياسة وعلى شروط الاستخدام، ومتى

**من هاتفك:**

- موقعك، فقط إذا سمحت بذلك، لنعرض لك الأماكن القريبة منك. نستخدمه لهذا البحث، ولا نحتفظ بسجلّ للأماكن التي كنت فيها.
- رمز الإشعارات، لنرسل الإشعارات إلى هاتفك
- بيانات تقنية لازمة لحماية حسابك: أوقات تسجيل الدخول، ونوع الجهاز، وعنوان الإنترنت (IP) لمحاولات تسجيل الدخول

لا نطلب جهات اتصالك أو صورك أو الميكروفون أو الكاميرا. ولا نجمع بيانات بطاقات الدفع: عند الإطلاق لا يقبل التطبيق أي مدفوعات.

- **الشركات التي تعمل لصالحنا**، بالقدر الذي تحتاجه فقط:

## 5. كم نحتفظ ببياناتك

نحتفظ ببيانات حسابك ما دام حسابك موجودًا.

يمكنك أن تطلب حذف حسابك من داخل التطبيق في أي وقت، ثم يُكمل فريق دعم E7GZ الحذف. وعند اكتماله:

- يُحذف اسمك ورقم موبايلك وبريدك وغيرها من بيانات التعريف من حسابك، ولا يمكنك تسجيل الدخول بعد ذلك؛
- تُلغى جلسات تسجيل الدخول ورموز الإشعارات الخاصة بك؛
- تُحذف تقييماتك؛
- يُحتفظ بسجلات الحجوزات السابقة وتاريخها دون بيانات التعريف الخاصة بك، لأن الأماكن وE7GZ تحتاج إلى سجلات صحيحة لما حدث.
